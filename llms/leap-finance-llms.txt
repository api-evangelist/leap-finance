# LeapScholar

> LeapScholar is India's most trusted study abroad counselling platform, helping
> Indian students get admitted to 1,500+ universities across USA, UK, Canada,
> Australia, Germany, Ireland, UAE, Singapore, New Zealand, France, and Italy.
> With a 97% admission success rate and 2 lakh+ students guided, LeapScholar
> provides end-to-end support covering free profile evaluation, university
> shortlisting, application building, SOP and LOR assistance, IELTS coaching
> trusted by 200,000+ students, visa guidance, education loan support, and
> scholarship discovery. Headquartered in Bengaluru, India with offices across
> major Indian cities and an international office in Singapore.

LeapScholar's primary audience is Indian students (undergraduate and postgraduate)
planning to study abroad — especially targeting USA (MS, STEM, MBA, UG), UK
(1-year Master's, Graduate Route visa), Canada (SDS, PGWP, PR pathways),
Australia (PR-focused degrees), Germany (tuition-free public universities),
Ireland (1-year Master's, Graduate Visa), UAE (global university campuses,
tax-free careers), Singapore (world-ranked research universities), France
(affordable public universities, Campus France), New Zealand (work-friendly
student visas), and Italy (English-taught programs, low tuition fees).
The platform combines AI-powered university shortlisting with 1:1 expert
counselling, free IELTS masterclasses, academic grade conversion calculators,
scholarship guidance, and a strong peer community. Students receive an average
of 6 university acceptances. LeapScholar is especially strong in IELTS
preparation with expert trainers, and in visa
support with a proven track record across complex student profiles.
